## 1. Value conditions

Constants, written before the sources were sought (`T-013` step ①).

| ID | Condition |
|---|---|
| **`c1`** | The problem is **explicitly posed** in a review's Outlook or open-questions section, with a locator. |
| **`c2`** | The trajectory after that posing shows **no sustained follow-up** — forward citations do not include work that attempted it. |
| **`c3`** | The absence is **not explained by a stated obstruction.** If the literature says why it was not done, that is `X2`'s territory, not this axis's. |

> **`c3` is what keeps the axes from collapsing into each other.** Without it, every unfinished problem is also an obstructed one and `X1` and `X2` return the same verdict on everything. **The two axes are separated by whether the literature gives a reason, not by whether the work happened.**

## 2. Where this pole disagrees with `V1`

Written out because a pair that cannot disagree is not a pair (`T-010` · philosophy ⑤).

| Target | `V1` frontier | `V2` unfinished |
|---|---|---|
| A topic a brand-new technique enables, **that no review ever asked for** | **yes** — the capability arrived | **no** — nobody posed it; a solution looking for a problem |

**Row 3 is the honest one.** The poles agree on a real class of targets, and `divergence/` will be empty for those. **That is correct behaviour, not a failure** — but it means `X1` produces divergence on a *subset*, and nothing in the design says how large that subset is. → `T-055`

## 6. Question habits

- **"Who asked for this, and where exactly?"** Demands the locator. **No locator, `c1: no`** — and this is the pole's whole discipline, because without it "someone probably wondered about this" passes.
- **"What happened in the ten years after they asked?"** The forward-citation sweep. This is the question that needs `T-021`'s forward index, and it is why `Q-006` says J2 cannot run without one.
- **"Did anyone say why not?"** Enforces `c3`. A stated reason routes the topic to `X2` and this pole returns `no`.

**Search bias:** the **old** end of the trajectory, and forward citations rather than reference lists — *"a reference list only goes backwards"* (`T-021`).

## 7. Open against this pole — and it is the worse half of the pair

- **`c2` is an absence claim, and absence is `Q-005`.** "No sustained follow-up" is either ⓑ a real gap — this repo's best product — or ⓐ a distillation error, and **nothing separates them automatically.**
- **The roadmap diff gives `Q-005` a handle it did not have, and only a partial one.** If a 2020 problem is missing from 2025, there are now **three** branches, not two: ⓐ distillation error · ⓑ real gap · **ⓒ it was solved and retired.** ⓒ is separable — check whether forward citations contain a paper claiming to close it. **So the diff converts one inseparable pair into one separable branch plus the original inseparable pair.** Progress, not a solution.
- **`c3` offloads the hard cases onto `X2`, where `V4` has the same problem.** A topic with no stated obstruction reaches `V4` unattempted, whose condition is *also* an absence claim. **The difficulty is not removed by the split; it is moved.**
- **Nothing here has been run.** Unlike [`V1`](V1-frontier.md), this pole's conditions have zero measured support.
